Radio links for very weak signals.

Ordinal Research is a small UK research company. We develop modulation and coding for radio links that have to keep working when the signal is very weak, at long range and low power.

Our results so far come from simulation and over-the-air tests on development radios. We'll publish technical results once they have been independently measured.

Ordinal was founded in 2026 by Theo Godfrey.
